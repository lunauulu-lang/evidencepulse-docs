What the scores mean

Readiness hints are deterministic metadata checks. A label or link is only an indication that evidence may exist; it is not proof that a test, attachment or compliance requirement was verified. The project overview is a sample, not an exhaustive audit or a release certification.

Who it is for

Jira team leads, project managers, QA teams and release coordinators who need visible, explainable issue-documentation checks.
